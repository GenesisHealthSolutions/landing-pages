Why This Matters

GLP-1 Works.
But Not Without This.

GLP-1 medications are among the most effective fat-loss interventions to date. STEP 1 showed semaglutide users losing 14.9% of body weight. SURMOUNT-1 showed tirzepatide users losing up to 22.5%.

The number most prescribers never discuss: 25–40% of weight lost on GLP-1 is lean muscle — not fat. GLP-1-driven deficits suppress muscle protein synthesis. While GLP-1 therapy alone achieves weight loss, the combined protocol of resistance training + EAA supplementation + GLP-1 therapy positions you to lose fat while attempting to preserve the muscle mass that supports your operational readiness and long-term health.

Up to 38% of weight lost on GLP-1 therapy is lean body mass, not fat. Adequate protein intake and resistance training are recommended by four major medical societies to mitigate this loss alongside GLP-1 therapy. (Mozaffarian et al., Am J Lifestyle Med, 2025 — PMC12125019)
